# The Meal Plan writes to reality once, at confirmation

The Meal Plan is a guide, not an execution: confirming it is the single moment it may write to
the Shopping List, and after that nothing derived from the plan changes on its own. The
alternative — deriving the Shopping List live from `remaining planned cooks − pantry` — is
self-healing and keeps "what can I cook" and "what must I buy" mathematically identical, but it
means the plan silently rewrites a list you also edit by hand, and it recalculates after the one
moment that matters (the shop trip) has already passed.

When the Pantry drifts from what the plan assumed — an off-plan Cook, a portion eaten early — the
plan surfaces the affected Recipe as not Cookable and offers a Top-Up. Acting on it is always the
user's choice.
